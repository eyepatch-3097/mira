# Mira — Turn Website Traffic Into Guided Conversations (AI Chatbots + Analytics)

Mira helps SaaS and D2C teams turn “lost browsing” into **guided product discovery** and **higher intent leads**.

Instead of users bouncing after scanning a homepage, Mira:
- understands what they’re trying to do,
- answers using your approved data,
- suggests the right pages/products,
- captures leads when users ask to connect,
- and shows you what’s happening across all your bots.

---

## Who Mira is for
Mira is built for teams that care about:
- **website engagement** (reduce drop-offs)
- **product discovery** (help users choose the right solution)
- **leads + intent** (capture high-quality inbound)
- **visibility** (analytics across conversations, not just clicks)

Common users:
- D2C founders + growth teams
- SaaS founders + marketing teams
- CX / Support teams
- Agencies managing multiple clients (multiple agents)

---

## The Mira User Story (End-to-End)

### 1) Add your Data Sources (the “truth” Mira can use)
Before creating a bot, you add the information Mira is allowed to use.

Mira supports multiple data source types, for example:
- **Website**: your pages, services, product/category pages, policies, etc.
- **Documents / Sheets**: FAQs, pricing tables, internal product notes, catalogs
- **Custom knowledge**: manual inputs you want the bot to know

Why it matters:
Mira does **not hallucinate** by default.
It answers using your sources, and if something is not present, it will respond appropriately.

✅ Outcome: Your knowledge base is ready.

---

### 2) Create an Agent (your chatbot)
An Agent is a complete chatbot you deploy on a website.

When creating an agent, you define:
- **Bot name + description**
- **Greeting message**
- **Brand UI** (icon, colors, chat appearance)
- Which **data sources** the agent uses, and for what purpose

Mira also supports “roles” for sources (how they’re used):
- Website Guidance (navigation + page suggestions)
- Contact Data (how to reach you / call intent)
- Support FAQ (help + troubleshooting)
- Document Referral (gated or ungated info)

✅ Outcome: Your chatbot is configured to behave like your brand.

---

### 3) Test in the Preview (before going live)
Mira includes a Test view where you can simulate user conversations and inspect behavior.

In preview, you validate:
- Does it answer from the correct sources?
- Does it recommend the right pages?
- Does it trigger lead capture only when needed?
- Does it correctly refuse irrelevant questions?

This is where you tune your sources and agent setup until the flow is clean.

✅ Outcome: Your bot works as expected before deployment.

## What Mira actually does during a conversation

Mira detects intent and routes the response accordingly.

### A) Product discovery + company questions
If a user asks:
- “What do you do?”
- “Which service is right for me?”
- “I need help with CAC / conversions / retention”
Mira:
1. checks your website + configured sources,
2. responds in a helpful structured way,
3. suggests relevant pages to continue exploring.

✅ Result: Users don’t drop off — they get guided.

---

### B) “How do I reach out?” → Lead Capture flow
If the user asks to connect (high intent), Mira:
1. immediately opens a simple form:
   - **Name**
   - **Email**
2. confirms submission
3. tells them someone will reach out

Mira keeps this flow intentionally minimal and fast.

✅ Result: lead captured at the moment of intent.

---

### C) Document-specific / named reference queries (optional gating)
If a user asks for something specific:
- “Do you have your pricing sheet?”
- “Show the catalog”
- “I want the document about X”
Mira checks the relevant document sources.

If it exists:
- Mira can optionally request name/email before revealing it (gated access)

If it doesn’t exist:
- Mira says it can’t help with that.

✅ Result: controlled access + clean expectations.

---

### D) Completely irrelevant questions
If a question is unrelated to the bot’s configured sources, Mira politely declines:
- “I can only answer based on our configured sources…”

✅ Result: no nonsense answers, higher trust.

---

## 4) Deploy the Agent (make it live on any website)
When you’re ready, Mira provides a deployment page that gives you:
- clear “how to install” instructions
- a copy-paste script tag

### Deployment method
You paste a single script inside your website’s `<body>` (or before `</body>`):

- Mira appears as a floating icon in the bottom-right corner
- clicking it opens the chat widget

✅ Result: bot goes live without rebuilding the site.

---

## 5) Conversations: full history of live chats
Once deployed, Mira stores:
- every chat session
- every message
- session metadata (agent, time, lead state)

You can view:
- conversation list (filterable)
- conversation detail (full transcript)

✅ Result: you can actually see what users are asking.

---

## 6) Analytics: what’s working and what’s not
Mira’s dashboard shows insights across bots, filterable by:
- date range
- agent selection

### KPI cards
- **Agents live**: how many bots are currently active
- **Data sources live**: how many unique sources active bots are using
- **Chat sessions**: number of unique sessions in the date range
- **Leads submitted**: lead captures completed

### Graphs & visuals
- **Chat sessions over time** (line chart)
- **Intent distribution** (pie chart)
- **Unanswered mood indicator**
  - 😊 good (low unanswered)
  - 😐 meh (medium)
  - ☹️ bad (high unanswered)

This gives you a fast read on:
- what users care about
- what they’re trying to do
- what your bot fails to answer
- which sources you need to improve

✅ Result: a feedback loop to improve engagement and conversion.
